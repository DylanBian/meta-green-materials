Meta Green Materials Ltd. is a UK materials science and engineering company. We develop and advance structural, functional and sustainable materials for demanding applications.

Through research, characterization, process development and collaboration with industrial partners, MGML works to move scientific concepts toward practical engineering use.

02 / CIRCULARITY
RECOVER / REFORM

Asphalt
rejuvenation

Project literature describes the MGM Two-Component Asphalt Rejuvenator for hot-recycled asphalt and mixtures containing reclaimed asphalt pavement (RAP). Its stated targets balance low-temperature crack resistance, resistance to moisture-induced damage and high-temperature deformation resistance; dosage and results depend on mixture design, RAP content and project conditions.

FOUNDER · DIRECTOR & CHIEF SCIENTIST

Dr. Junwei
Xie.

Dr. Junwei Xie is a materials scientist and engineer with a Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering from Tsinghua University. His research spans advanced structural and functional materials, high-entropy alloys, metal–ceramic composites and materials characterization, with a focus on how processing and microstructure shape performance. Before establishing Meta Green Materials Ltd. in the UK in 2025, he spent more than a decade in road and bridge engineering. At MGML, he leads work connecting materials research, process development and engineering applications.
