01ABOUT MGML

Materials thinking,
grounded in engineering.

Meta Green Materials Ltd. (MGML) is a UK materials science and engineering company. We develop structural, functional and sustainable materials for demanding service conditions, from road surfaces that resist icing to processes that recover strategic metals.

Our work runs from materials design and characterization through process development to engineering validation. MGML holds the patents and the registered MetaGreenMaterials® trademark behind its technologies, and licenses them to industrial partners who manufacture and apply them.

01 / TRANSPORT

Anti-icing road
materials

  • Anti-icing
  • Snow-melting
  • Pavement materials

Phase-change components store ambient heat and release it at the road surface, a lotus-leaf micro-nanostructure repels water, and an elastic structure resists ice formation. Applied as a spray or roller coating (open to traffic in two hours), as an additive mixed at the asphalt plant, or with solar-assisted heating.

02 / CIRCULARITY

Asphalt
rejuvenation

  • Reclaimed asphalt
  • Cracking resistance
  • Stability
  • Resource efficiency

Our MetaGreenMaterials® Two-Component Asphalt Rejuvenator (MGM) restores aged binder in hot recycled asphalt. It toughens the mix at low temperature while keeping it stable at high temperature, so a properly designed mix can contain up to 80% reclaimed asphalt pavement (RAP). Typical dosage is 0.10–0.30% of mixture mass.

03 / CONNECTIVITY

Intelligent
road markings

  • Durable
  • Machine-readable
  • Optical enhancement
  • Contamination resistance

We are developing metamaterial road markings for automated driving and V2X: optical microstructures designed to stay readable in rain and low light, self-cleaning superhydrophobic surfaces, and infrared-responsive structures for machine vision. A nonmagnetic covering layer keeps them compatible with magnetic markers, RFID and infrared barcodes.

04 / MATERIALS RECOVERY

Critical
materials

  • Separation
  • Refining
  • Purification
  • Resource recycling

We develop separation, refining and purification processes for precious, platinum-group and strategic metals, from Pt, Pd and Rh to Nb, Ta, Ga and In. Working from complex feedstocks and secondary resources, we aim to produce high-purity metals, compounds and powders for semiconductors, energy and aerospace.

FOUNDER · DIRECTOR & CHIEF SCIENTIST

Dr. Junwei
Xie.

Dr. Junwei Xie is a materials scientist and engineer with a Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering from Tsinghua University. His research spans advanced structural and functional materials, high-entropy alloys, metal–ceramic composites and materials characterization, with a focus on how processing and microstructure shape performance. Before establishing Meta Green Materials Ltd. in the UK in 2025, he spent more than a decade in road and bridge engineering. At MGML, he leads work connecting materials research, process development and engineering applications.
